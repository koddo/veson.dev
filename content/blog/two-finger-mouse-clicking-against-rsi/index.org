#+title: Two-finger mouse clicking against RSI
#+date: <2026-04-05 Вс>

Around three years ago I got scrolling-induced RSI, which is Repetitive Strain Injury.

Basically, I hurt my hand by using the mouse wheel too much.

I was obsessed with trying to learn more about how transformers work in LLMs. I had been scrolling papers, code, and explanations back and forth for many days on end, which I don't normally do, because the context I want or have to understand at once is usually much smaller.

I couldn't use the mouse any more, so I had to go to a store and try out an ergonomic vertical mouse. Luckily, the staff didn't object and let me do this. I didn't buy it, it didn't help at all, the hand still hurt with every movement, including clicking.

Then after a bit of experimenting I came up with the following: I swapped the mouse buttons in the settings, it's now configured as left-handed, and I click the primary button with two fingers:

[[file:hand-on-mouse-position-adjusted-for-clicking-with-two-fingers.jpg]]

It looks weird, but it reduces strain on my hand significantly.

The index finger moves to the regular position for a brief moment to click the secondary button. Unfortunately, this is not for gaming nor 3d modeling. 

[[file:hand-on-mouse-position-usual.jpg]]

I have to add that the mouse should not be flat, because otherwise it's too much strain on the pinky, when I lift it for movement.

Also I bought an external numpad, to scroll with my other hand.

It took many months for my hand to heal, but it did, and I'm sharing this because I haven't found anything similar online. Hopefully, this would be helpful for someone.
